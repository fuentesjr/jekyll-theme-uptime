WPM = 238 — the average adult silent-reading rate for English *non-fiction*, from Brysbaert (2019), "How many words do we read per minute? A review and meta-analysis of reading rate" (Journal of Memory and Language; 190 studies, 18,573 participants). Fiction averages ~260 wpm; we use the non-fiction figure because the posts here are prose. The older, widely-cited 300 wpm is an overestimate per that meta-analysis. `words / 238` gives raw minutes; `ceil` rounds a partial minute up, so a 1.2-minute read shows as "2 min" and a sub-minute post never shows "0". (238.0 forces float division — Liquid's divided_by floors when both operands are integers, which would wrongly drop the fractional minute.)
